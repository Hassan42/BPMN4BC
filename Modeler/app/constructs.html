The modeling elements of BPMN4BC, what they mean and how to use them in the modeler.

A BPMN4BC diagram has one blockchain pool (the smart contracts) and one or more participant pools (the people or systems that send transactions). Only on-chain elements may be placed in the blockchain pool, and the only way into it is a message flow from a send transaction task to a receive transaction task. Everything a receive transaction task triggers, up to the next wait, runs as one transaction: it either completes or is reverted as a whole.
